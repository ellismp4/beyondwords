      Troubleshooting

      Quick fixes for common call problems. Your visit stays open while you troubleshoot.

      Check your sound

      Press the button. You should hear a short spoken sentence.

      I can't hear the patient
      1. Check your device volume and that it is not muted.
      2. Check headphones or speakers are connected.
      3. Ask the patient to type a message or use the gestures while you fix it.
      The patient can't hear me
      1. Make sure your Mute button says "Mute", not "Unmute".
      2. Allow microphone access if the browser asks.
      3. Use "Type a message" to share what you were saying.
      Video is not working
      1. Check the camera button says "Turn camera off".
      2. Allow camera access and close other apps using the camera.
      3. Continue by voice or by typing if video cannot be fixed.
      The call is slow or freezing
      1. Move closer to your router or switch to a wired connection.
      2. Pause other streaming or large downloads.
      3. Turn your camera off to reduce bandwidth.
      4. Refresh the page. Notes and the patient profile are saved.
      A communication tool is missing
      1. Tools shown during the call match the patient's profile.
      2. The patient can change them by editing their profile on their page.
      3. Updates appear here when the patient saves them.
