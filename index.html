HELP

Having trouble?

Try these steps one at a time. You can come back to your visit at any time.

Check your sound

Press the button. You should hear a voice say a short sentence.

I can't hear my provider
  1. Turn up the volume on your device.
  2. Check that your device is not on silent or muted.
  3. If you use headphones, unplug and plug them in again.
  4. Press "Play test sound" above to check your speakers.
My provider can't hear me
  1. On the call, check the Mute button says "Mute" (not "Unmute").
  2. Allow microphone access if your browser asks.
  3. Move to a quieter spot if you can.
  4. You can always type a message or use your communication tools instead.
My video isn't working
  1. Check the camera button says "Turn camera off" (not "Turn camera on").
  2. Allow camera access if your browser asks.
  3. Close other apps that might be using your camera.
The call is slow or freezing
  1. Move closer to your Wi-Fi router.
  2. Ask others in your home to pause streaming or downloads.
  3. Turn your camera off to help the call run better.
  4. Refresh this page. Your profile is saved and will still be here.
I can't find my communication tools
  1. Only the tools you chose in your profile appear during a visit.
  2. On the home page, press "Edit My Profile".
  3. Tick the tools you want, then press Save. They show up the next time you join a visit.

Still stuck?

Ask a family member or caregiver to help, or contact your provider's office.
